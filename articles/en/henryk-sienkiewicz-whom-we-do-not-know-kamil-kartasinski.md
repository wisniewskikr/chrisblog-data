# Content

## 1. Tatar (and not only) roots

- The Sienkiewicz family traced its origins to the Lithuanian Tatars.
- Father – Józef: a landowner who eventually sold his village estate and moved to Warsaw.
- Mother – Stefania (née Cieciszowska): wrote poetry for her own drawer.
- Siblings:
  - Kazimierz: a participant in the January Uprising, killed near Orléans during the Franco-Prussian War.
  - Henryk: born 5 May 1846 in Wola Okrzejska.
  - Aniela.
  - Helena: a nun who translated novels and adapted her brother's works.
  - Zofia: wrote popular works for common readers.
  - Maria: died in childhood.
