# Content

- Being an active citizen goes beyond voting and paying taxes — it means taking responsibility for public issues.
- We cannot rely solely on the state to solve all societal problems; individuals must also play a role.
- Gabriel Marmentini outlines four key ingredients for becoming a change-maker:
  1. **Political knowledge** — understanding how systems and institutions work.
  2. **Plural dialogue** — engaging with diverse perspectives and fostering open conversation.
  3. **Civic empathy** — developing the ability to understand and share the concerns of others in society.
  4. **Social protagonism** — taking active initiative and ownership in addressing public challenges.
