# Content

## I. Presto – a child's haste

### Magical Sarmatia
- Frédéric Chopin was born in Żelazowa Wola.
- His parents were Mikołaj and Justyna.
- Mikołaj taught French literature at the Warsaw Lyceum; previously he had been a tutor in Żelazowa Wola, had fled the French Revolution as a teenager, and had worked as an accountant in a tobacco factory and as a law enforcement officer.
- Justyna ran the household of her relative, Countess Ludwika Skarbek, in Żelazowa Wola; she was the housekeeper; her maiden name was Tekla.
- The family lived in the annex of the Ossoliński Palace in Warsaw.
- Elder sister: Ludwika.

## Light and darkness
- 1812 – Napoleon marches on Moscow.
- Two more daughters were born: Izabela and Emilia.
- Mikołaj joined the Freemasons when Frédéric was 2 years old.
- After the defeat in Russia, Napoleon was left with 10% of his soldiers; he later lost at Leipzig and Waterloo.

## The Saxon puppet
- The Chopin family moved to the Saxon Palace.
- Frédéric is 6 years old and already learning to play.

## Power trembles
- The Czech Żywny became Frédéric's teacher.
- Frédéric gave a concert of Christmas carols.

## Exaltations and excursions
- The Lyceum moved to the Kazimierz Palace; the Chopin family moved with it.

## Secrets and masters
- At the age of 7, Frédéric composed a piece.
- At the age of 8, he gave a concert at the Radziwiłł residence.
- Żywny taught Chopin Bach, who was underappreciated at the time – known merely as an ordinary musician from Leipzig.

## The wonder child
- At the Chopins', stories were told and Frédéric composed melodies to accompany them.
- Frédéric was invited to perform at the homes of Warsaw's most important figures.
- His father eventually put a stop to these visits, citing his son's poor health, so that Frédéric would have time to compose.

## Music works miracles
- At the age of 13, Frédéric began taking lessons from Elsner.
- He learned to play the organ with Würfel.
- Frédéric's improvisations became a family tradition.

## II. Vivace. A lively young man

### Kujavian notes
- Chopin stays in Kujawy, at the Romocki manor house called Oborowo.

## Living pictures
- Frédéric went to watch the highlanders.

## Miasmata
- Frédéric learns Italian.
- He enrols in the Main School of Music.
- His sister Emilka dies.
- Frédéric travels to Duszniki-Zdrój to restore his health.

## The brink of despair
- Chopin goes to Kowalew for the holidays.
- After Emilka's death, the Chopin family moved to the Krasiński Palace.
- Frédéric sets out with his host on a business trip, reaching as far as Gdańsk.

## Music without borders
- Chopin's friend Jaś Białobłocki dies.
- The governess Rozalia became pregnant; Frédéric was accused of being the father.

## The grand world and the little muse
- Chopin visits Berlin.

## The little slipper
- Chopin improvises variations on the theme of a little slipper.

## Musical paradises
- Chopin graduated from the Main School of Music in Warsaw.
- He travelled to Vienna, stopping in Kraków and Wieliczka on the way.
- On the return journey they travel through Bohemia to Kalisz.

## Capital cafés
- Chopin frequented the café called Dziurka.
- He befriends Juliusz Słowacki.

## The Copernicus Monument Building Committee
- Chopin teaches Stanisław Moniuszko.
- At a scholars' gathering, Chopin meets Joachim Lelewel and Aleksander Fredro.

## The magnetism of ladies
- Chopin performs a concert at the National Theatre consisting entirely of his own compositions.
- He waits for Konstancja Gładkowska, a singer.
- Another singer: Henrietta Sontag.
- For the summer, Chopin goes to Żelazowa Wola.
- In October he gives his last concert in Poland.

## III. Busily across Europe

### A stuffy All Souls' Day
- His parents sent him out into the world, fearing the upheavals in Poland.
- Chopin is 21 years old.

## Uproar and darkness
- Chopin feels unwell in Vienna.

## Aerumna like a coffin
- Chopin moves to Württemberg.
- The November Uprising breaks out in Poland.

## IV. The virtuoso's moderation

### On the Parisian cobblestones
- Paris was the artistic capital of the world.

## Entrancement
- Chopin teaches Countess Potocka.
- He performs a concert with 13 artists.
- He meets Liszt, the Hungarian composer.
- Cholera strikes Paris.

## Ambition and embarrassment
- Chopin is 24 years old and has more and more pupils.

## The charming beauty of ugliness
- Chopin spends a month with his family in Carlsbad.
- He is in Dresden, en route to Leipzig, where he meets Maria Wodzińska.
- He meets George Sand.
- He proposes to Maria, but after a few years the engagement is broken off.

## A good beginning to something bad
- George Sand is Aurora Dupin, Baroness Dudevant.
- She is 6 years older than Chopin.

## V. A quiet pace with Aurora

### Amorous overtures
- George Sand's father abducted her children.
- Sand has a romance with the tutor of her son – Mallefille.

## Words and painting music
- Grzymała is Chopin's friend.
- The painter Delacroix is another friend of Chopin's.
- Chopin and George Sand consummate their relationship.

## Autumn on the island
- Chopin and Sand travel to Majorca, via Barcelona.
- A storm rages on Majorca and Frédéric falls ill.

## Winter on Majorca
- They moved to the monastery in Valldemossa.
- It rained a great deal.
- They left Majorca.

## A frenzy of raptures
- Chopin is back at Nohant with Sand.
- Sand buys Chopin a piano and gingerbread.
- Maria Szymanowska was an outstanding pianist and composer; Mickiewicz married her daughter Celina.

## Incomprehensible restless rains
- Chopin and Sand have two apartments close to each other in Paris.

## Two Russias and one prophet
- Chopin converses with Custine.

## Faust descends to the Forefathers' Eve
- Sand writes about the three national bards; Chopin helps her with the part concerning Mickiewicz.

## Three gentlemen in the atelier
- Sand's son Maurice begins his apprenticeship with Delacroix.

## The great sorrows of the great emigration
- Mickiewicz moves from Lausanne to Paris.
- A literary duel between Mickiewicz and Słowacki takes place at Januszkiewicz's.

## Romantic synthesis of the arts
- Chopin is ill.

## A study from nature
- Balzac corresponds with Madame Hańska.

## The fat one, the thin one and the sexy one
- Sand and Chopin talk about Balzac.

## The poetry of music
- Towiański proclaims himself a prophet; Mickiewicz and Słowacki belong to his sect.

## God's cohort
- The Towianists urge Chopin to join, but he is an individualist.

## The peaceful, cheerful countryside
- Witwicki visits Chopin and Sand.

## Space for art
- Chopin and Sand go for a ride – she on a horse, he on a donkey.

## Poland yet
- At a gathering, Chopin plays the Mazurka Dąbrowskiego.

## Parisian duets, trios and quintets
- Chopin meets Dumas.

## Pride, disgrace and guilt
- Chopin's father dies.
- Chopin falls ill.

## VI. Slowly seeking freedom

### Coda to youth
- Chopin and Sand go to Boussac.

## Madmen and aristocrats
- Chopin performs at the Czartoryski Palace.

## The queen's gambit
- Sand wants to marry off her daughter Solange to Clésinger, a sculptor of dubious reputation.

## Powerlessness and ardour
- Sand tends to the ailing Chopin, but now out of pity, not love.

## Solitude slowly approaches

### Goodbye, little doll, adieu, madame
- Chopin and Sand part ways; Chopin suffers from increasingly serious tuberculosis.

## The fighter and art
- Chopin talks with Goszczyński.

## The February Revolution and the war of worlds
- 1848 – revolution in France.
- Mickiewicz went to Italy to form legions.
- Chopin embarks on a tour of England and Scotland.

## VIII. Far and wide in Great Britain

### A castle of paper and mist
- Chopin stays with his former pupil Stirling.

## The four seasons in autumnal darkness
- Chopin is increasingly ill.

## IX. A serious condition, heavy thoughts

### Synaesthesia
- Chopin learned of the death of Juliusz Słowacki, who was one year older than Chopin.
- Chopin received a painting from Michałowski, a very talented artist.

### A fatalist among the Romantics
- Chopin moves to Chaillot.

## The nest of vipers
- Chopin's sister Ludwika visits him in Paris with her family.

## An examination of conscience
- A priest urges Chopin to return to the Church.

## Black flowers
- Chopin died.

## X. Without a steady pulse

### Sentimental education
- Chopin's funeral; Sand did not come.

## Relics and a miracle
- Ludwika saved Chopin's letters.
- Ludwika smuggled Chopin's heart into Poland.

## Fire burns, earth absorbs
- The Russians destroyed Chopin's piano.
