# Content

- We all have an **advice monster** — an inner impulse that rushes to offer suggestions before truly understanding the situation.
- There are three types of advice monsters: the **Tell-It**, the **Save-It**, and the **Control-It**.
- Giving advice too quickly often means solving the wrong problem or disempowering the person you're trying to help.
- The antidote is to **stay curious longer** — ask questions instead of jumping to solutions.
- Useful questions to ask:
  1. What's the real challenge here for you?
  2. And what else?
  3. What is the real challenge here?
  4. What do you want?
- People need to find their own answers — your job is to ask, not to provide your answers for them.
- Staying curious strengthens relationships and makes you a more effective helper.
