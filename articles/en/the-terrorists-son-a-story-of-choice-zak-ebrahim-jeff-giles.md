# Content

## I. November 5, 1990

- Father, Sayyid Nosair, shot and killed a rabbi.
- During the escape, both the father and a postal inspector were shot in the neck.
- The rabbi died.

## II. Today

- In 1993, the father helped organise the first World Trade Center bombing from his prison cell.

## III. Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

- The mother was raised by a single Christian mother.
- She married an atheist, they had a daughter, and later divorced.
- She converted to Islam because she needed faith; she met the author's father during her conversion.
- The father was groundlessly accused of rape.

## 4. 1986. Jersey City

- The father was electrocuted at work; he returned to work but gradually became a fundamentalist.
- The war in Afghanistan broke out; Muslims asked for help fighting Russia, and Azzam arrived seeking funds.
- Another fundamentalist — the Blind Sheikh — came to the United States.

## 5. January 1991. Rikers Island Correctional Facility, New York

- To ordinary people the father was a murderer; to extremists he was a hero.
- Gifts poured in for the family.

## 6. December 21, 1991. New York State Supreme Court. Manhattan

- The jury found the father guilty only of lesser offences, not murder.
- The family moved more than 20 times; the gifts grew smaller over time.
- At Attica prison, families are allowed to spend a weekend with inmates.

## 7. February 26, 1993. Jersey City, New Jersey

- The first World Trade Center bombing took place.
- Police linked the van to Salameh — a postal worker who had promised to marry the author's sister.
- The father and the Blind Sheikh were involved; the attack was organised by Yousef, a Pakistani national.
- The bombing was an act of revenge for US support of Israel.

## 8. April 1996, Memphis, Tennessee

- The mother divorced and remarried — a Muslim amateur boxer.
- The stepfather beat the author for even the slightest transgression.

## 9. December 1998, Alexandria, Egypt

- The family moved to Egypt.
- The stepfather continued to beat the author.

## 10. July 1999, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

- The author got a job at an amusement park and revealed his true story to two friends.
- He watched Jon Stewart's programmes — serious issues presented in a humorous way.

## 11. Epilogue

- How a terrorist is made:
  - Find someone vulnerable.
  - Isolate them.
  - Then teach them to hate.
