# Content

## 1. Giving

- Those who are most persuasive are the ones who first offer help and support, and do so in a clearly unconditional way.

## 2. Exchange

- When we cannot repay our benefactor, we behave in a friendly way towards others.
- Instead of saying "No problem", one can say "Here we help each other whenever needed".

## 3. Gifts

- It is best to ask what the person wants.
- It is better to buy an expensive gift of the simple kind than a cheap one of the elaborate kind.

## 4. Collaboration

- Focus on what unites.
- At work, when you want to convince others of something, it is good to first make a draft and ask colleagues and managers for feedback — they will start to identify with the project.

## 5. Breaks

- Sad people are willing to buy at a higher price and sell at a lower one.

## 6. Compromises

- People very often say yes to a smaller request after they have said no to a bigger one.

## 7. Knowledge

- People expect experts to show them the way, but experts must be subtle.
- You can also ask someone to introduce you before a meeting.

## 8. Admitting weakness

- A person who performs excellently but admits a mistake is perceived as more likeable.

## 9. Asking for help

- People are far more likely to say "yes" than you expect them to be.
- When you ask someone for help and are turned down, the pain is short and sharp.
- When you do not ask for help, the pain is less sharp but long-lasting.

## 10. Conversations

- Conversations can help expand your network of contacts.

## 11. Humanising

- If you want to present something, find a story that will bring your narrative to life.

## 12. Liking

- Like is far more often drawn to like than opposites are attracted to each other.

## 13. Compliments

- It is good to find a positive quality in a person and mention it.

## 14. Labelling

- It is worth giving someone a positive label and then telling them about it (e.g. "I know you can do this").
- People try to behave in accordance with that label.

## 15. Giving a reason

- It is worth saying "because".

## 16. Fulfilling commitments

## 17. Carrying out intentions

- The pattern: if…, when…, then…

## 18. Comparing

- The last candidates and the last proposals are very often the ones chosen.

## 19. Following an example

- People follow examples — hence the popularity of reviews.

## 20. Loss

- The ratio of loss to gain is 2:1.

## 21. Finishing in great style

- We do not remember the whole, only the most important moments, especially the endings.
