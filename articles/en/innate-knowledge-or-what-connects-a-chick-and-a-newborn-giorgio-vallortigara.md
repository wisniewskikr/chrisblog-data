## Foreword

- Nativists: we possess innate knowledge; the mind resembles a Swiss Army knife – a set of specialised tools.
- Empiricists: we are born as a blank slate and acquire knowledge solely through experience.

## Chicks and newborns:

- They prefer face-like objects.
- They prefer self-propelled objects.
- Separate processes take place in separate brain hemispheres.
- The preferred stimuli probably act as an "innate teacher" – children instinctively know from whom to learn.
- Chicks instinctively freeze when a predatory bird moves away and flee when it approaches.
- Sexual imprinting: an individual seeks a partner neither too similar nor too different from itself.
- Humans have an innate level of light perception.
- Animals have an innate ability to count.
- Animals have an innate geometric sense; spatial orientation relies on:
  - place cells,
  - head-direction cells,
  - boundary vector cells,
  - grid cells.
