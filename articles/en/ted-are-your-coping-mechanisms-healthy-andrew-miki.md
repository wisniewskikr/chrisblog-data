# Content

- Just as physical activity supports our physical health, talking and reflecting supports our mental health.
- A key question to ask yourself: what do you do when you feel stressed or sad?
- A second key question: do those feelings get better or worse over time with your chosen coping strategies?
- Many common coping mechanisms — such as procrastinating, overusing smartphones, or endlessly scrolling social media — feel relieving in the moment but actually deplete us over time.
- These habits can gradually erode self-confidence and resilience rather than building them up.
- Psychologist Andrew Miki shows how to identify unhealthy coping patterns and replace them with strategies that genuinely strengthen mental well-being.
