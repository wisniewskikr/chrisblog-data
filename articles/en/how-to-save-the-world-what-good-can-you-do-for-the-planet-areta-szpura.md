# Content

## Introduction

- Use things as long as possible
- Buy responsibly, locally, and only as much as you need
- Reduce plastic use, meat consumption, and car travel
- If you can, do it yourself
- Share
- Dispose of waste consciously
- Educate others
- Don't sit idle – take action

## 1. Science doesn't lie – learn the truth

- Aerobic decomposition produces carbon dioxide; anaerobic decomposition produces methane, which then breaks down into carbon dioxide
- If the temperature rises by more than 2°C compared to the pre-industrial era, a cascade of changes will be triggered
- CO₂ emission targets: reduce by 40% by 2030, reach net zero by 2050
- Climate knowledge website: http://naukaoklimacie.pl
- From 1970 to 2014, vertebrate populations declined by 60%, freshwater species by 83%, and in South and Central America by 89%
- Only 9% of plastics are recycled worldwide – recycling alone is insufficient
- The solution is a circular economy: share, repair, reuse, process, and recycle
- Phone companies based on recycling: Fairphone and Apple
- The Ellen MacArthur Foundation works on environmental care

## 2. The method of small steps

- Act starting with yourself and take small steps
- Key areas: reducing waste, transport, avoiding palm oil, electronic tickets, avoiding leaflets
- Use the Ecosia search engine

## 3. Plastic is forever

- When making new plastic packaging, usually no more than 30% of recovered regrind is used
- 5 types of waste are sorted: plastics, glass, paper and cardboard, metals, organic waste
- PSZOK (Municipal Selective Waste Collection Point) accepts problematic waste such as electronics and batteries
- Since 2013, mixing waste is no longer allowed; garbage trucks have compartments and collect sorted fractions separately
- Materials are divided into plastics and bioplastics, compostable and non-compostable; oxo-degradable/oxo-biodegradable materials break into small fragments and are not suitable for recycling
- Practical alternatives: metal water bottle, wooden toothbrush, tooth powder or toothpaste in an aluminium tube, homemade coconut milk, wooden dish-washing brushes, compostable bags

## 4. Do it yourself – learn to take care of yourself and your home

- Eco-friendly detergents: Simple as That brand, Betterland eco shop
- Basic ingredients for homemade cleaning products: baking soda, washing soda, spirit vinegar, salt, citric acid
- Additional ingredients: plant-based soap flakes, sodium percarbonate, essential oils
- Natural cosmetics: raspberry seed oil, helichrysum oil, tamanu oil, grape seed or hemp oil, honey, clay, oat flour, rose water, rice water

## 5. Fashion for years

- Buy vintage clothes and from local brands
- Join Fashion Rebels
- Natural laundry powder: washing soda, borax, soap flakes, essential oil
- Clothes donation: http://ubraniadooddania.pl

## 6. Live more plant-based

- Replace meat with legumes
- Use a compost bin
- Beeswax wraps are a great alternative to single-use cling film

## 7. Explore the world – without guilt

- Fly as little as possible
- When possible, offset your ecological footprint

## 8. Act wisely

- Effective altruism (EA) – how to act effectively; main areas: fighting poverty, animal suffering, the future of humanity, new areas
- EA tips: choose recommended career paths, support the most effective organisations, learn more

## 9. Be the change

- Companies worth noting: Nago (sustainable fashion brand), Svoje (local cosmetics), 4szpaki
