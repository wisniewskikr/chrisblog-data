# Content

- In the United States, work is very often tied to personal identity — the cultural message is that you should "do what you love."
- This approach can be limiting: when your job becomes who you are, losing it or struggling with it threatens your entire sense of self.
- There is an alternative: you can work *in order to* do the things you love outside of work.
- One practical strategy is dividing your time into distinct, unbreakable "bubbles" — for example: a work bubble, a play-with-the-kids bubble, a learning bubble, and so on.
- Keeping these bubbles separate helps protect your sense of meaning and identity from being defined solely by your professional life.
- Reclaiming your time and sense of purpose beyond the office is possible — and it starts with rethinking the role work plays in your life.
