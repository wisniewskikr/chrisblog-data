# Content
- In 1970, Henrik Stiesdal built an early wind turbine, laying the groundwork for companies like Vestas and Denmark's rise as a wind energy pioneer.
- Over the past two decades, the wind power industry has grown at an extraordinary pace — one rotation of the world's most powerful turbines can charge more than 1,400 cell phones.
- Denmark has developed the ability to convert surplus wind electricity into hydrogen, enabling energy storage and export.
- Despite being the largest oil producer in the European Union, Denmark plans to shut down its oil industry entirely by 2050.
- Dan Jørgensen presents a concrete plan to transition Denmark to a fully fossil-free future powered by wind energy.
