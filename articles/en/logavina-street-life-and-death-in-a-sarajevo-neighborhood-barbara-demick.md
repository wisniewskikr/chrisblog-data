  a city famous for its tolerance and cosmopolitan charm. The outbreak of war turned
  the lives of its residents upside down — Bosniaks, Croats and Serbs who had until

  deprived of heating and electricity, with increasingly limited food and water supplies,
  they must now fight every day for their own survival and that of their loved ones.
  They must defend themselves not only against snipers' bullets, but also against
  an equally dangerous enemy — hatred toward neighbors and former friends who can
