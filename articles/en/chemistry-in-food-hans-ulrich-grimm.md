# Content

## 1. Faster ageing

- E249–251: nitrates and nitrites.
- E211: benzoate.
- Phosphates: may damage the heart; described as the new cholesterol.

## 2. Scratching in the throat

- Glutamate enhances flavour – used, among other places, in Asian restaurants.
- Bitter-taste blockers deceive the body: they prevent it from being warned about potentially harmful substances, which is why they are considered harmful.

## 3. The bitter truth

- Aspartame – a common sweetener.
- Stevia – a new sweetener.

## 4. Tooth remnants in the mouth

- Tooth erosion: citric acid present in juices can cause it.
- Colourants do not add up quantitatively but accumulate in the body.

## 6. The secret ingredient

- Ingredients that enhance the perception of sweetness or saltiness.
- Flavour enhancers are declared as food products, allowing the label to be "cleaned" (Clean Label) – the product appears natural even though it has been chemically processed.
