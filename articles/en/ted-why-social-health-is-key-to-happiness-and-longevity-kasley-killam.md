# Content

- We often focus on physical and mental health, but tend to neglect **social health** — our sense of belonging and connection with others.
- Social health has concrete, measurable benefits for overall well-being and longevity.
- Feeling connected is not just a nice-to-have; it may be the missing key to a longer and happier life.
- A practical framework for maintaining social health:
  1. Aim for **5 social interactions** per week.
  2. Nurture at least **3 strong, close relationships**.
  3. Dedicate at least **1 hour per day** to meaningful connection.
- Be **active and intentional** about your social health — treat it as seriously as diet or exercise.
