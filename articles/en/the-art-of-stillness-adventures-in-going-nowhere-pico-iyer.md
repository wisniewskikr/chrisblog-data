## Introduction. Going nowhere

- Cohen joined a Buddhist monastery in the USA and contemplated silence.
- Access to information and freedom of movement were once the greatest luxury.
- Today, the greatest reward is liberation from that access and the ability to remain still.

## I. The crossing to nowhere

- Going nowhere is not about turning away from the world.
- It is about stepping away from it from time to time – in order to see more clearly and to love more deeply.

## II. A map of stillness

- Ricard – son of a scientist and a painter – was called the happiest man on earth thanks to meditation.
