# Content

## 1. The square tips the tower and the American gambit

### Army of the New Model

- There is a leader and a court that controls access to him.
- The square is society, and the tower is power.
- Total reliance on the Americans irritates them and they stop treating us as partners.
- The American gambit: they went to the USA so that the Americans would influence the Polish government.
- The most important element of the military is morale and determination.

## 2. What is this all really about

- When the poles of the world shift, a transitional state occurs and wars break out.
- The goal of war: agency, i.e. effectively influencing others to pursue one's own interests.
- The basis of proper functioning is movement – from it comes exchange and money, competitiveness and rivalry arise, and from there it is one step to war.
- In 1793 the telegraph was invented – the paths of transport and information diverged.
- The life of a state is governed by the law of force.
- Levers: supply chain, raw materials chain, communication corridor.
- The struggle for: spheres of influence, norms/standards/rules, narratives and ideology, new ideas.
- The productive centre of gravity (Germany, France, China) favours more regulation; the financial centre of gravity (USA and the United Kingdom) favours less regulation.

## 3. Dread – a dream in reverse

- Russians believe that in the territories of Ukraine, Lithuania and Belarus, either Poles or Russians can rule.
- Russian strategies: drawing the enemy in, defending the border, shifting borders westward and creating buffer zones.
- Belarus's position is of strategic importance to Poland.
- What matters now is readiness and communication – military mobility and flexibility of response.

## 4. War is a method

- State strategy translates into competitive strategy.
- A sensor system transmitting data in real time is needed.
- Manoeuvre allows one to win a war, but is currently difficult due to sensors.
- War of attrition is the next stage.
- Article 5 will be triggered, but not immediately.
- It is not equipment that fights, but soldiers – war is a method.
- Jan Sobieski defeated the Cossacks and Tatars by method.
- Artificial intelligence can support military operations.
- The military is a system, it is an art.
- In the event of war, the aim is to increase asymmetry.

## 5. War like ballet

- Currently, politicians dominate the military.
- The outcome of a war is determined by the way it is conducted, not by technology – examples: Algeria, Indochina, Afghanistan.
- Equipment should only be discussed after strategy has been addressed.
- The defence debate in Poland is a ritual imitation devoid of understanding.
- The legal system is not ready for war: there is no definition of hybrid warfare.
- There is no mandatory participation of officials in war games.
- Help is given only to those who are able and willing to defend themselves.

## 6. August and before

- In Poland there are many meetings that lead to nothing.
- The accession of Finland and Sweden to NATO is good news for Poland.
- Key decisions: defending the Suwalki Gap or Warsaw, permanent American presence east of the Vistula, an own situational awareness system.

## 7. Captain Bomba and others

- People are the most important asset.
- Officers and non-commissioned officers must be capable of independent thinking.
- Command and teaching posts only after several years of service in line units.
- Rotation of training staff.
- Sequences: observation – orientation – decision – action.

## 8. The war we may (perhaps) have to fight

- China and the USA demand that others adapt.
- A ban on selling components to China.
- A ban on selling sand to Taiwan.
- Thermonuclear weapons somewhat curb the desire for wars.
- A military coalition of Intermarium states.
- Poland could try to join Nuclear Sharing.
- Nuclear weapons: good for deterrence, bad for coercion.
- It must be signalled that one is ready to fight even after a nuclear attack.
- A nuclear programme that can be converted into a nuclear weapon relatively quickly.
- Tube and rocket artillery combined with good target reconnaissance are important, as are ammunition reserves and efficient servicing.
- Ships are currently "white elephants" – they can be eliminated first.
- We need Polish Space Forces; otherwise we will always have to ask for information.
