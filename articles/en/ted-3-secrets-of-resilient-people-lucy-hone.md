# Content

- Resilience is not a rare gift — it can be learned. Understanding how resilient people think and act is the first step.
- **Strategy 1: Accept that bad things happen.** Suffering and loss are a natural part of life. Recognising this helps you stop feeling singled out and start adapting.
- **Strategy 2: Choose your focus carefully.** Resilient people direct their attention toward things they can actually change, rather than dwelling on what they cannot. Negative emotions tend to stick, while positive ones fade quickly — so actively looking for the good in a situation takes deliberate effort.
- **Strategy 3: Ask yourself — is what I'm doing helping me or harming me?** This simple question acts as a compass, guiding decisions and habits toward recovery rather than self-destruction.
