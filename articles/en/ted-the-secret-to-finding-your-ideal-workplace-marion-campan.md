# Content

- When looking for a new job, company culture matters more than salary or job title.
- Observe the recruitment process itself — how a company treats candidates reflects its internal culture.
- Ask the right questions during interviews to uncover what a workplace is really like:
  1. What kinds of people get promoted here?
  2. What are your core values?
  3. When did you last receive feedback here?
- The answers (and how they are given) reveal whether the company's values align with your own.
- Choosing a workplace based on culture increases your chances of truly thriving in the role.
