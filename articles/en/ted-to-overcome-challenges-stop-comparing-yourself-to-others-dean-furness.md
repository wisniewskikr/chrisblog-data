# Content

- Bad things happen, but they don't have to ruin your entire day — the key is to move on immediately instead of dwelling on them.
- When you let go of negative moments quickly, you create space for good things to happen.
- Over time, this raises your "personal average" — your overall sense of how well things are going.
- Wheelchair athlete Dean Furness lost the use of his legs in an accident, but found a powerful new mindset in the aftermath.
- The core of that mindset: stop comparing yourself to others and focus on your own progress instead.
- Improvement comes little by little — small, consistent steps forward matter more than giant leaps compared to someone else's journey.
