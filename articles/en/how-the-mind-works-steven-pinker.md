## 1. Standard equipment

- Two laws govern thought (1748): contiguity – ideas that often appear together become associated in the mind; similarity – when two ideas are associated, whatever was associated with the first becomes associated with the second
- The problem of the individual: neural networks record features as neurons; they easily distinguish classes and subclasses (e.g. animal/horse) but struggle with individuals (e.g. horse/Ed)

## 3. Revenge of the nerds

- Evolution is about goals, not means – the goal is to pass on genes; intelligence is not a goal but a means
- Intelligence is a gadget, like a trunk – we have it because in prehistory its benefits outweighed its costs
- Correct theory: natural selection
- Flawed theories: Lamarckism (use and disuse of organs and inheritance of acquired traits), macromutation (an immediately functional new mechanism from a copying error), random drift

### The blind programmer

- Natural selection does not deliver information but builds brains
- Evolution and learning are two different things, but they can occur simultaneously
- Learning changes the problem of finding a needle in a haystack into the problem of finding a needle when you are already close

### Instinct vs intelligence

- The human mind is built from ready-made elements – instincts
- Species develop at the expense of others
- Humans occupied the cognitive niche
- Humans have an advantage because they attack animals that can defend themselves only evolutionarily in subsequent generations

### Why us

- Possible reasons for intelligence: we are visual creatures (we see "what" and "where"); we live in groups (we must be smarter than others); we have hands; hunting (better nutrients for the brain)
- Dating: entertainment, food, feeling; at the start lots of entertainment and little feeling – when feeling replaces entertainment it is no longer a date; food is there throughout

### The modern Stone Age family

- At some point in our history we were probably an endangered group – down to about 10,000; hence our biological similarity
- Culture spreads like infectious diseases; evolution created the psyche and that is how the mind is explained

## 6. Hotheads

- Disgust prevents eating something that could be harmful
- Children probably narrow down what is disgusting in the first two years of life
- Ethnic food taboos: they most often forbid what enemies eat, to avoid fraternising with them
- Wealth is like health: its absence makes one unhappy, but having it does not guarantee happiness
- Problem: twice as many negative feelings as positive ones; loss is felt far more acutely than gain
- Campbell's quote: The direct pursuit of happiness is a recipe for an unhappy life
- Listening to emotions instead of reason is sacrificing a long-term goal for short-term gain
- Types of altruism: kin altruism and reciprocal altruism
- Feelings associated with altruism: liking (someone who will reciprocate), anger (someone cheated), gratitude (someone who did reciprocate), sympathy (a desire to help), guilt (someone might catch me cheating)
- Distinguishing genuine from feigned feelings builds trust or distrust

## 7. Family values

- Murder rates (per million): 0.5 – Iceland; 10 – Europe; 25 – Canada; 50 – USA and Brazil
- Murders in the USA: 1/4 family (2–6% biological relatives), 1/4 complete strangers, half acquaintances
- No society can be simultaneously fair, free and equal
- Personalities: sociable/withdrawn (extraversion–introversion); anxious/composed and self-satisfied (neuroticism–stability); cautious/reckless (conscientiousness–carelessness); agreeable and trusting/rude and suspicious (agreeableness–antagonism); bold/submissive (openness–lack of openness)
- Research shows that children are raised by their peer group, not by their parents
- Whether family members feel sexual attraction depends on whether they are raised together; people cannot sense shared genes – they assume that people they live with share the same genes
- Sex is a defence against parasites and pathogens
- We reproduce sexually because of mitochondria: if they came from both a man and a woman they would compete; men supply only DNA, without mitochondria
- The world features male competition and female choice
- Women are more selective than men, but once they consent there is no reason to think they respond less strongly to sexual stimuli
- Affairs: women – because a lover surpasses or complements the husband; men – because the woman is not their wife
- Infidelity: men are more hurt by physical infidelity, women are more hurt by emotional infidelity
- The purpose of friendship is to rescue you in hard times when it would not be worth anyone else's effort
- War is waged by: humans, chimpanzees, dolphins, and possibly bonobos

## 8. The meaning of life

- The psychology of art is the psychology of status
- Art is like religion – it has no adaptive function
- We laugh 30 times more often when we are in the company of others
- Perhaps laughter was a signal of mock aggression – that this is play, not a real fight
- Three components of humour: incongruity, substitution, disparagement
- The possible purpose of humour is to maintain friendship; dominance–subordination exists between people, but people also want friendship – they then use humour to reject dominance
- Religion is a technique for success
