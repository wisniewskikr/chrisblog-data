## 2. Chance and destiny

- Determinism: a predetermined path.
- Tendency: a path in a given direction, but with choices available.
- Rigid (mosaic) development: if a cell is destroyed at an early stage, the corresponding body part does not form; this is the case in insects.
- Flexible (regulative) development: every cell can potentially become any part of the body; this is the case in mammals.
