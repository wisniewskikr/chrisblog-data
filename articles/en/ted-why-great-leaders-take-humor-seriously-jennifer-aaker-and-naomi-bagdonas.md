# Content

- Many leaders believe they must be serious at all times to be taken seriously — research shows this is a misconception.
- Leaders who use humor are perceived as more creative and competent.
- Humor is a powerful tool for building bonds, strengthening relationships, and increasing resilience.
- Our brains have a tendency to notice what we are primed to look for — a mindset shift can help us spot more opportunities for levity.
- The key insight: humor is not about amusing yourself, but about connecting with and delighting others.
- Jennifer Aaker and Naomi Bagdonas teach these ideas in their course at Stanford's Graduate School of Business.
