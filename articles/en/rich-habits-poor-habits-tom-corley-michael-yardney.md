# Content

## I. Why we can teach you how to get rich

### Introduction. The rich are getting richer – and you?
- The income gap is growing, which is a bad sign.
- Our thoughts trigger emotions, emotions lead to actions, and actions have consequences.

## 1. Who is Tom Corley?
- A certified accountant who analysed the habits of the rich and the poor.

## 2. Who is Michael Yardney?
- A property investor from Australia.

## 3. Why we want you to become wealthy
- Financial independence eliminates 67% of serious life problems.
- Financial levels: 1. Financial instability; 2. Financial stability; 3. Financial security; 4. Financial freedom; 5. Wealth.

## II. How to develop a wealthy person's mindset

### 4. You are the master of your financial destiny
- Each of us belongs to one of four categories: employees, the self-employed, investors, and business owners.
- Linear income: you receive only one pay cheque for your work.
- Passive income: you do the work once and get paid for it repeatedly.
- The rich keep getting richer because they put their money to work in order to grow their assets.
- Wealth is the fruit of capital, not income.

## 5. How to get rich. A few key concepts
- You should aim to be rich in cash and assets.
- The rich have more assets generating a steady passive income.
- Types of investors: 1. Spenders; 2. Savers; 3. Passive investor; 4. Active investor; 5. Professional investor.
- Investor steps: self-education, accumulating capital.
- You must live below your means and save money.
- Convert income into assets that will generate passive income.

## 6. Developing a wealthy person's mindset
- 40% of our actions are habitual.
- What you think about money matters.
- All external stimuli pass through a filter – our beliefs.
- Taking action is essential.

## 7. Why most people will never be rich
- The strongest emotion we feel about money is fear.
- You don't need to know everything – just enough to start investing.

## 8. Thirty-nine ways of thinking characteristic of wealthy people
- Recommended book: Napoleon Hill, *Think and Grow Rich*.
- The third law of thermodynamics: you have to give money away to receive money.

## 9. Rich habits that support success
- The poor have an emotional relationship with money; the rich have a logical one.

## 10. The secret of delayed gratification
- The secret of the rich: patience and postponing the moment of satisfaction.
- It helps to focus on something else.

## III. Rich habits and poor habits

### 11. The rich habits programme
- Types of fate: lucky chance; unlucky chance; favourable fate (we have influence); unfavourable fate (we have influence).
- 67–80% of wealthy people are self-made.
- The rich work longer because they enjoy it.
- The rich strive for self-improvement.
- Habits are crucial and ordinary.
- The best energy comes from passion.
- The rich have effortless concentration – they love what they do; it lasts longer than forced concentration.
- The rich are able to prioritise tasks.
- The rich control their thoughts and emotions.
- You need to sketch out a plan for your ideal life.
- Goals are steps towards achieving dreams.
- The rich exercise moderation.
- There is a struggle within each of us between acting and procrastinating.
- Mantra: "Do it now".
- Rule: save 10% of income and live on the remaining 90%.
- The rich control their words and emotions.
- Happiness is not a goal but a state of mind.

## 12. Summary of the habits of wealthy people
- Summary of the habits described in the preceding chapters.

## IV. Why the relationships and thinking of the rich matter

### 13. Models, mentors, strategists
- We see the world through the lens of our mental model.
- It is best to learn from those who have already travelled the road you are just starting out on.
- A person is like a cup: if the cup is too small for a large fortune, the wealth will not stay – you need to be a bigger cup.
- It is good to have a team.

## 14. What every parent should teach children about making money
- Teach saving.
- Teach patience and waiting.
- True wealth is what remains when you lose your money and material possessions.
- Understand the difference between wants and needs.
- Children adopt habits from their parents.

## 15. A few closing remarks
- Put the habits you have learned into practice.
