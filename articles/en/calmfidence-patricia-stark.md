# Content

## Introduction

- Calmfidence is not something we are born with – it is something we learn and can develop in ourselves.

## I. Calmfidence in everyday life

### 1. Calmfidence boosters

- Every morning it is worth asking yourself: Am I balanced or overwhelmed? Motivated or discouraged? Is my energy positive or negative? Am I in control? Do I want to feel the way I feel?
- Our choices, thoughts and actions affect body, mind and soul.
- Calmfidence boosters:
  1. Smart attention management – what do you focus on?
  2. Gratitude
  3. Happiness
  4. Growth mindset
  5. Segmenting
  6. Seize the day
  7. Setting real value
  8. Spiritual grounding
  9. Building your own team
  10. Morning habits
- It helps to replace the word "I must" with "I have the opportunity" – e.g. "I have the opportunity to go to work" – and feel grateful for that opportunity.

## 2. Calmfidence killers

- Calmfidence killers:
  1. Shadow people – assume the best, not the worst
  2. Inner critic
  3. Worrying
  4. Perfectionism
  5. Procrastination – putting things off
  6. Defining yourself through the external world
  7. Comparing yourself to others and despair
  8. Oversensitivity about oneself
  9. Self-sabotage

## 3. Self-care and calmfidence

- Take care of your emotional, physical, mental and spiritual needs.
- It is good to quiet down and simply do nothing.
- Helpful tools include: having a special place to unwind and ensuring good sleep.

## II. Calmfidence and resilience

### 1. Calmfidence and failure

- When a problem stands in your way, try to solve it in different ways – there is a good chance that one of them will work.

## 2. Calmfidence and courage

- Courage is not about not feeling fear, but about overcoming it.
- Courage is the decision that you want something more than you fear it.
- It is worth working on the skill of carrying on a relaxed conversation that amuses or interests the other person.

## 3. Calmfidence tools

- Calmfidence tools:
  - Credit card – write down your strengths, things you are grateful for, and your goals
  - Breathing – slow belly breathing
  - Inspirations
  - Rituals
  - Visualisation
  - Thorough preparation
  - Breaking action into small steps
  - Practice
- If you feel no discomfort, you are probably not growing.
- Respond to situations with deliberate action rather than a quick, unconsidered reaction.

## 4. Calmfidence and self-image

- Self-image is influenced by: age, body, unrealistic expectations and posturing.

## 5. Calmfidence and goals

- When pursuing goals: start acting, do not stop, and maintain a high standard.

## III. Calmfidence in communication

### 1. The inner voice of calmfidence

- Three things that influence the inner voice:
  1. Fear of failure
  2. Negative comfort zone
  3. Self-monitoring

## 2. The outer voice of calmfidence

## 3. The eyes of calmfidence

- Healthy eye contact – brief eye contact lasting 2–5 seconds.
- It is worth scanning the other person's face for non-verbal signals.
- Nodding or shaking your head and keeping your chin up are desirable.
- Looking up and down is acceptable; darting eyes – looking left or right – is not advisable.

## 4. Face to face with calmfidence

- A smile is a sign of a friendly and positive attitude, openness and confidence.
- Your face must tell the same story as your words.
- Basic emotions: fear, anger, sadness, joy, disgust, contempt, surprise.
- Stages of reaction: intention or accident → unconscious reaction (body language or facial expression) → conscious reaction → words.

## 5. Calmfidence and body language

- Interpreting body language is not the same as mind-reading – its purpose is to lead to asking better questions.
- Gesturing is encouraged.

## 6. Calmfidence and public speaking

- A presentation is not about you – it is about your audience.
- Nervousness is natural, but it is worth replacing it with excitement.
- Stage fright works like a rocket launch – it lasts about 20 seconds and then levels off.
- People need: food, shelter and companionship.
- A presentation is a gift and the conclusion is its wrapping and ribbon – most people remember three things, so that is how you should finish.

## 7. Calmfidence and job interviews

- Prepare thoroughly.
- Prepare questions about the company.

## IV. Natural support for calmfidence

### 1. Natural remedies supporting calmfidence

- Remedies that ease stress and anxiety: lemon balm, cherries, bananas, cinnamon, chamomile, and others.

## 2. Exercises to strengthen calmfidence

- Exercises: snowball, sack of potatoes.
