# Content

- Personality is often described using the **OCEAN** model: **O**penness, **C**onscientiousness, **E**xtraversion, **A**greeableness, **N**euroticism.
- Personality is shaped by three forces: genetics, the influence of society and culture, and what makes each person truly individual.
- We are defined by our **personal projects** — the things that matter most to us and are unique to each individual.
- For the sake of these personal projects, we can act against our natural traits — stepping outside our usual character.
- Example: a generally agreeable person may become assertive and confrontational when fighting for a loved one's life in a hospital setting.
- This suggests personality is more flexible and malleable than we typically assume.
