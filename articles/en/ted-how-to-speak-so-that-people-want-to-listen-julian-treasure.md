# Content

- The talk focuses on how to speak so that others genuinely want to listen.
- **Seven habits to avoid** (they make people stop listening):
  1. Gossiping
  2. Judging
  3. Negativity
  4. Complaining
  5. Making excuses
  6. Lying
  7. Dogmatism
- **Four cornerstones of powerful speech** (acronym HAIL):
  1. Honesty – be clear and straightforward
  2. Authenticity – be yourself
  3. Integrity – be what you say you are
  4. Love – wish others well
- **Vocal tools** that shape how we are heard:
  1. Pitch – the highness or lowness of your voice
  2. Timbre – we tend to prefer voices that are rich and warm
  3. Prosody – varying your intonation rather than speaking in a flat monotone
  4. Pace – the speed at which you speak
  5. Volume – how loudly or quietly you speak
- Before important events, warm up your voice with vocal exercises.
