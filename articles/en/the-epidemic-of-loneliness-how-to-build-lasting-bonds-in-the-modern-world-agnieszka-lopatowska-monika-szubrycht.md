# Content

## 1. Loneliness in a crowd

- A person who likes themselves does not feel lonely.
- Intimacy with another person means being curious about more than trivial details – this also prevents loneliness.
- One should be separate yet open to others.
- Causes of loneliness: childhood (lack of a secure bond), culture (technology), a profound life failure.
- The opposite of freedom is a bond: someone ties us down, or we have a bond with someone.
- The net is ambiguous: one is caught in a net, or one lives in a network.
- Capitalism is good, but not predatory – that requires mindfulness and monitoring.
- The remedy for loneliness is self-awareness and openness to others.

## 2. Loneliness on one's own

- Relationships mean responsibility that we do not want to take on.
- It is better to spend 10 mindful minutes with a child than 2 inattentive hours.
- Relationships with others reflect the relationship with oneself.

## 3. Loneliness in a relationship

- Empathy: I see you and I believe you.
- In a relationship we know someone and fail to notice how they change – updates are necessary.
- Every relationship serves a purpose; during a lifetime we have 3–4 relationships, sometimes with the same person.
- Jealousy is a longing for something.
- Shame is fear of judgement.
- Tenderness is the younger sister of love.
- We are made up of good parts and less good parts.

## 4. Loneliness in motherhood

- Nurture yourself beyond the role of mother.
- The problem may not be that something was taken from us, but that we ourselves stopped striving for something.
- Connect four: connect with four people every day.
- Finding balance means constantly staying in motion.

## 5. Loneliness in non-heteronormativity

- Sexual orientation emerges early: around age 13–14 in girls, around 15–16 in boys.
- Psychosexual orientation is shaped during foetal life.

## 6. Loneliness in narcissistic personality disorder

- Narcissistic personality disorder (NPD) is a mental disorder, but not a mental illness.
- A narcissist is a lonely person who lacked love in childhood.

## 7. Loneliness in childhood

- Loneliness occurs when a person feels misunderstood.
- One should not view a child through stereotypes.
- A rejected child is a lonely child.
- Adults must be adults – emotionally stable.

## 8. Loneliness in adolescence

- 13% of children are cared for solely thanks to the 800+ benefit.
- Digital hygiene needs to be taught – perhaps starting in antenatal classes.
- Poland ranks 2nd in Europe in child suicide.
- The theory of small steps: seek out small happinesses.

## 9. Loneliness in illness

- Emotional intelligence means examining another person's emotions and choosing the best behaviour for them; empathy is part of it.
- As on an aeroplane: take care of yourself first, then of the ill person.

## 10. Loneliness in addiction

- Addiction often begins out of fear of loneliness – we want to belong to a group.
- Deterioration: accelerated breakdown of the body or of social contacts.
- Addiction triggers: HALT (Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired).

## 11. Loneliness in trauma

- Loneliness is the inability to form a bond, i.e. to enter into a relationship.
- A relationship: I + You + Bond.
- The way out of loneliness: first plants, then animals, finally people.

## 12. Loneliness in caregiving

- Autism is diverse.
