# Content

## 1. What is this book about?

- Women and men share 45 identical pairs of chromosomes, but the 46th pair differs: women have XX, men have XY.
- Differences between the sexes also stem from hormones and sexual characteristics.
- Primary male sexual characteristics: testes, which produce semen and testosterone.
- Secondary: scrotum, vas deferens, penis.
- Tertiary: body hair, voice, narrow hips and broad chest.
- Testosterone makes men self-confident, and thereby witty and frivolous.

## 2. Testosterone

- Testosterone's father is cholesterol; cortisol (the stress hormone) also uses it, and cortisol is stronger than testosterone.
- Testosterone comes in two forms: free (2–3%) and protein-bound.
- Peak secretion occurs between 4–6 a.m.; by evening the level can be 30% lower.
- Thanks to oestrogen, women need the right mood for sex: evening, the smell of a fireplace, candlelight, a glass of wine.
- Enemies of testosterone: stress, physical inactivity, a polluted environment (air, water, food).
- Oestrogen from contraceptive pills passes through urine into sewage and then into drinking water consumed by men.
- Sex is better in couples where household duties are divided; women prefer sex with a man holding a drill rather than washing-up liquid.
- Xenooestrogens are plant-derived oestrogens; they are found in soy, legumes (beans, peas, broad beans, lentils), flaxseed, pumpkin and onion.
- Currently 20% of men produce an unnaturally low amount of sperm.
- Many plastic food containers contain BPA; some countries have banned its use.

## 3. How to recognise declining testosterone?

- Symptoms of testosterone deficiency: lack of desire for sex, general fatigue, irritability.

## 4. Libido

- Libido: a state of intense desire to possess someone in order to satisfy sexual needs.
- Causes of libido problems can be organic (pharmacological treatment) or psychological (psychotherapy).

## 5. Potency

- Potency: a man's ability to have sexual intercourse; its culmination is an erection.
- Premature ejaculation is treated with techniques (e.g. start-stop) or medications (tadalafil, fluoxetine).
- Erectile dysfunction: viagra helps.
- Normal intercourse lasts 4 to 8 minutes.

## 6. Depression

- Research shows a link between testosterone levels and depression.
- In Poland, more men die as a result of suicide than accidents.

## 7. Sport

- Sport raises testosterone levels, but excessive exertion (e.g. a marathon) lowers them.

## 8. Heart, the healthy pump

- Homocysteine is a problem for the heart; vitamins B9 and B12 help.
- Vitamins B9 and B12 are found in: lettuce, cabbage, spinach, broccoli and Brussels sprouts.
- Recommended tests: ECG and stress test, carotid artery sonography, cardiac echocardiogram, carotid and coronary angiography, blood tests (cholesterol, fasting glucose, haemoglobin AC1, homocysteine, uric acid, fibrinogen), computed tomography of the coronary and abdominal vessels.
- Types of stroke: haemorrhagic (hypertension causes a vessel wall to rupture) and embolic (an atherosclerotic plaque causes a clot in the heart, lungs or brain).

## 9. Just don't get fat

- Fat cells convert testosterone into oestradiol (a female hormone).
- Type 1 diabetes has a genetic basis; type 2 diabetes results from overeating.
- Apple-type obesity (fat in the internal organs) is more dangerous than pear-type obesity.

## 10. Psychosomatic diseases

- The body suffers because the soul suffers; the cause is an inability to cope with stress.

## 11. Alcoholism

- Alcoholism: cultural and social factors play an enormous role.
- Stages of alcoholism: 1) forgetting about problems, 2) psychological dependence, 3) physical dependence.
- Some ethnic groups are protected: Africans eliminate alcohol from the body extremely quickly, Asians do not tolerate it in larger quantities.

## 12. Conclusion

- Factors of health: mental hygiene, physical activity, proper nutrition.
- Genes: good genes are lucky; bad genes accelerate the losing battle for health.
- Characteristics of contemporary people: excessively high self-regard, inflated expectations, reluctance to accept criticism.
