# Content
- Buildings account for approximately 39% of global energy-related emissions; transportation accounts for about 23%.
- The four dominant building materials today are concrete, steel, masonry, and wood — all with significant environmental footprints.
- Architect Michael Green argues we need a fifth material: a new, natural building medium inspired by the structure of trees and plants.
- This material would be capable of sequestering carbon rather than emitting it during production and use.
- Green's vision aims to lay the groundwork for a construction industry that actively contributes to solving the climate crisis.
