# Content

- Love is a skill — it can be learned and developed, not just felt passively.
- Dan Harris experienced an on-air panic attack after years as an ABC News anchor, which pushed him toward meditation.
- Despite becoming a dedicated meditator and a self-improvement advocate, an anonymous survey from family, friends and colleagues revealed he was still often a jerk.
- This humbling feedback drove him to explore loving-kindness meditation — a practice focused on directing warmth and goodwill toward yourself and others.
- The science behind loving-kindness meditation shows it can boost emotional resiliency, reduce the power of the inner critic, and improve your relationships.
- Being kinder to yourself is not self-indulgence — it is a foundation for being kinder to the people around you.
- Small, consistent practices of self-compassion can make you measurably more pleasant to be around.
