# Content

## 1. Humans are animals
- The first human skill was cracking bones and eating the marrow.
- Other tools followed, such as those for digging up carrots.
- Then humans discovered fire – a force that did not come from their own bodies. Fire provided protection and the ability to cook: less time and effort spent on eating, smaller stomachs, larger brains.
- Besides Homo sapiens, other humans also lived on Earth: Neanderthals, Denisovans, the dwarfs of Flores, and others.
- Homo sapiens means "wise human" in Latin.
- 100,000 years ago, about 100,000 Homo sapiens lived in Africa.
- There are many species of bears, ants, and so on, but only one species of human – Homo sapiens eliminated all the others.

## 2. The superpower of sapiens
- Humans can cooperate in very large groups.
- A single person can form strong relationships with at most 150 individuals.
- Apart from us, only social insects (ants, bees, termites) can cooperate in large groups, but they always organise themselves in the same way.
- Our superpower is the ability to invent fiction, which allows large numbers of people to cooperate.

## 3. How our ancestors lived
- It is written in our genes: if you find something sweet, eat as much of it as fast as you can.
- Prehistoric people most often did not live in caves, but in camps on the savannah.

## 4. Where did all the animals go
- 50,000 years ago humans reached Australia – one of the most important events in history; from that point on, humans began to change the world.
- In Australia, humans wiped out many animal species; they later did the same in the Americas.
- When mammoths disappeared, other animals died out too – mammoths acted like ploughs in winter, maintaining the ecosystem.
