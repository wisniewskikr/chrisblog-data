# Content

- There are two fundamental mindsets that shape how we process information and defend our beliefs.
- The **soldier mindset**: the world is divided into allies and enemies, and the goal is to defend your position at all costs.
- The **scout mindset**: driven by curiosity and openness, the scout wants to understand the world as accurately as possible.
- Changing your mind as a scout is not a sign of weakness — it means you are updating your map based on new evidence.
- The key to better thinking is not focusing on logic alone, but on emotions: cultivating feelings of **pride** in being open-minded and **genuine interest** in discovering the truth.
- When your beliefs are challenged, ask yourself: do you yearn to defend what you already think, or do you yearn to see the world as clearly as you possibly can?
- Choosing the scout mindset leads to better decisions, stronger relationships, and more honest self-knowledge.
