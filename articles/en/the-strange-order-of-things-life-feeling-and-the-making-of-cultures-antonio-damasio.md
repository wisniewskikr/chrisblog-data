## Part I. On life and its regulation (homeostasis)

## 1. On the human condition

- Feelings tell the organism in a non-verbal way whether it is on a good or a bad path.
- Feelings disturb equilibrium, thus becoming catalysts for change.
- Feelings are representatives of homeostasis – the state of the organism.
- Feelings, as representatives of homeostasis, are catalysts for the reactions that gave rise to human cultures, including such intellectual inventions as: art, philosophical inquiry, religious beliefs, moral principles, justice, systems of political power, technology, and science.

## 2. In the land of unlikeness

- Metabolism: a process that requires the cell to draw as much energy as possible from sources in its environment, use it as efficiently as possible to rebuild worn-out machinery, and remove waste.
- Metabolism consists of catabolism (the breakdown of compounds to obtain energy) and anabolism (the building process in which that energy is used).
- Homeostasis: the desire to endure now and always.
