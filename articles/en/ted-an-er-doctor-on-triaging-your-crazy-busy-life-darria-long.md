# Content

- "Crazy busy" should never be a default state — it raises stress hormones and impairs brain function.
- ER doctors are trained to shift from "crazy mode" to **ready mode**, and you can apply the same approach to daily life.
- **Phase 1 — Prioritize ruthlessly (triage):** Assign everything a color: red (act immediately), yellow (less urgent), green (can wait), black (nothing can be done — let it go and move on).
- **Phase 2 — Prepare and plan:** Instead of being caught off guard, anticipate chaos and plan your response in advance:
  1. Plan ahead
  2. Automate recurring decisions
  3. Keep things in a consistent, dedicated place
  4. Reduce temptations and distractions
- **Phase 3 — Get out of your own head:** Crazy mode is fueled by a negative inner monologue and tunnel thinking.
- Shifting your focus to compassion — for others or yourself — broadens your perspective and breaks the tunnel vision.
