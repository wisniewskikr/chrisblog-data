# Content

## Everyone has resources

- We take shortcuts out of laziness or pressure.
- Humans tend toward negativity: one bad thing outweighs five good ones.
- Every person has values and resources (strengths).
- Procrastination is a battle between the limbic system (fear, anxiety) and the prefrontal cortex (impulse control).
- The tendency to put things off stems from lack of immediate results, the tedious or boring nature of a task, the effort or engagement required, and a sense of pressure.
- Procrastination is a habit, and habits can be changed.
- A habit consists of a trigger, a repeated behavioural pattern, and a reward.
- It is worth shifting one's mindset from "I must" to "I want to".

## Does work really ennoble?

- We build our sense of self-worth in the private, social, and professional spheres.
- Work provides a sense of financial autonomy and the opportunity for development.
- Work structures the day and has social value — it allows us to meet worthwhile people.
- The aspects of work are: organising life, economic, social, and developmental.
- Complaining temporarily relieves tension but does not address its cause, reinforces one's own bad mood, and causes a bad mood in others.

## What is failure?

- Failure should neither be dwelt upon nor ignored.
- It should be used as a source of learning.

## Mornings can be good

- It is worthwhile to start the morning with a gratitude practice.

## The Monday curse

- The weekend should be used for genuine rest.

## Setting boundaries

- We distinguish three zones: intimate, personal, and social.
- Our boundaries are closely linked to our sense of identity.

## Rest

- Laziness is connected to avoidance — we push the problem into the future.

## Holidays without disappointments

- A holiday should last at least three weeks.

## What next

- Emotions are like a wave: they build up, reach a peak, and then level off — we should allow them to go through this cycle.
- If we put a wall in the way of our emotions and prevent them from unfolding, it will end in chaos.
