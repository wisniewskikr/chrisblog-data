# Content

## Education is the ability to cope with everyday life situations.

### I. Fundamental techniques for dealing with people

### 1. If you want to gather honey, don't kick over the beehive

- Don't criticize, condemn, or complain.
- Criticism is pointless because it puts people on the defensive.
- Those in the wrong blame everyone but themselves.
- If you want to improve someone, start with yourself.
- People are often driven not by logic but by emotions.

## 2. The big secret of dealing with people

- Give honest and sincere appreciation.
- One of the most important human needs is the feeling of importance.
- The most important thing is building enthusiasm – it is done through appreciation and encouragement; criticism kills enthusiasm.

## 3. He who can do this has the whole world with him. He who cannot walks a lonely way

- Arouse in the other person an eager want.
- Don't talk about what you want – talk about what your listener wants and how to achieve it.
- See things from your listener's point of view as well as your own.

## II. Six ways to make people like you

### 1. Do this and you'll be welcome anywhere

- Show genuine interest in other people.
- You can make more friends in two months by being interested in others than in two years by trying to get others interested in you.

## 2. How to make a good first impression

- Smile.
- A smile is an act that says: I like you.
- Happiness is a matter of controlling your own thoughts.

## 3. If you don't do this, you are headed for trouble

- Remember names and surnames.
- A person's own name is more interesting to them than all other names put together.
- Good manners are made up of small sacrifices.

## 4. How to be a good conversationalist

- Be a good listener.
- Attentive listening is the greatest compliment you can pay a speaker.

## 5. How to interest people

- Talk about what interests your listener.
- The easiest way to a person's heart is to talk about the things that matter to them.

## 6. How to make people like you instantly

- Make your listener feel important – and do it sincerely.
- Always make the other person feel important.
- Do unto others as you would have them do unto you.

## III. How to win people to your way of thinking

### 1. You can't win an argument

- An argument will be your victory only when you avoid it.

## 2. How to make enemies – and how to avoid it

- Show respect for your listener's opinions; never say "You're wrong".
- Say instead: "I may be wrong, but it seems to me…".
- The little word "my" is the most important word to any person – we spend little time thinking things through, but when someone tries to take our opinions away, we defend them to the last.

## 3. If you're wrong, admit it

- If you are wrong, admit it quickly and emphatically.
- It is better to get in ahead of the other person and admit fault immediately.

## 4. A drop of honey

- Begin in a friendly way.

## 5. The secret of Socrates

- Get the other person saying "yes" as soon as possible.
- When talking, begin by emphasizing the things on which you agree.
- Try to get as many "yes" responses as possible and as few "no" responses as possible.

## 6. The safety valve in handling complaints

- Let the other person do a great deal of the talking.

## 7. How to get cooperation

- Let the other person feel that the idea is theirs.

## 8. The magic formula

- Try honestly to see things from the other person's point of view.
- When others are wrong, there is a reason – try to find it.
- If you want to persuade someone to do something, ask yourself why they would want to do it.

## 9. What everybody wants

- Show sympathy for the other person's ideas and desires.
- Formula: "I don't blame you for feeling as you do. If I were you, I would feel just the same".
- Sympathy is like a chemical neutralizer for the acid of bad feelings.

## 10. Everybody likes this

- Appeal to the nobler motives.
- Everyone does things for two reasons: a real one and one that sounds good.
- Appeal to the real reason.
- People are honest and mostly want to live up to their commitments.

## 11. What works in movies and advertising works in life too

- Dramatize your ideas.
- Nowadays it is not enough to state a truth – you must make it vivid, interesting, dramatic.

## 12. When nothing else works

- Throw down a challenge.
- The desire to excel is a powerful motivator.

## IV. Be a leader

### 1. If you must find fault

- Begin with sincere praise and appreciation.
- It is easier to hear criticism after receiving genuine praise for what we have done.

## 2. How to criticize and not be hated for it

- Call attention to other people's mistakes only indirectly.
- Don't use the word "but".

## 3. Talk about your own mistakes first

- Before criticizing others, admit your own mistakes.

## 4. No one likes to take orders

- Ask questions instead of giving orders.
- Instead of ordering, it works well to say: "You might want to consider…".

## 5. Let the other person save face

- Let people save face.

## 6. How to spur people on to success

- Praise every improvement, no matter how small.
- Praise inspires change.

## 7. Give a dog a good name

- Give people a fine reputation to live up to.
- We respond to how others perceive us.

## 8. How to make people believe they can change

- Encourage others to improve.
- Praise what is good and make faults seem easy to correct.
- Let the other person know you believe in their abilities and that the task is easy.

## 9. How to make people gladly do what you want

- Make the other person happy about doing the thing you suggest.
