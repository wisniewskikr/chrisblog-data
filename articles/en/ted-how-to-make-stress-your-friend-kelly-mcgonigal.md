# Content

- If you don't believe stress is harmful, it actually doesn't damage your health — how you *think* about stress changes even the physiological response of your body.
- Stress also makes you more social: it can trigger the urge to connect, empathize, and help others.
- Oxytocin plays a key role here — it is released during stress and acts as a natural antidote to adrenaline, making the body's stress response less damaging.
- There are two main factors that make stress harmful:
  1. How you *think* about stress (believing it is bad for you).
  2. How you *act* during stress (isolating yourself instead of caring for others).
- Taking care of others during stressful times can neutralize the negative effects of stress on the body.
