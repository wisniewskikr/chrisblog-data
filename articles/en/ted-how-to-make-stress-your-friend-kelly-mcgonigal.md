# Content

- If you don't view stress as harmful, research suggests it won't actually damage your health.
- How you *think* about stress matters — your mindset can change even the physiological responses of your body.
- Stress also has a social side: it can make you more empathetic, more willing to help, and more inclined to connect with others.
- Oxytocin plays a key role here — it is released during stress and acts as a natural antidote to adrenaline, making the stress response less harmful to the body.
- Two main factors determine whether stress is bad for you:
  1. How you *think* about stress.
  2. How you *act* — caring for others during stressful times can reduce stress's negative effects.
