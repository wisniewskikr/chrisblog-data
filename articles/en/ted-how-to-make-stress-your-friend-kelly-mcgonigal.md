# Content

- Research shows that how you *think* about stress influences how stress *affects* you — if you believe stress is not harmful, it is less likely to harm you.
- Before a stressful situation, telling yourself that stress helps you focus and perform better leads to a much healthier physical response.
- Stress also makes us more socially inclined: it motivates us to seek out social support from others.
- Taking care of others and building social connections creates resilience against external crises.
- Key takeaway: your reaction to stress depends on how you think about it and how you act on it — think positively about stress and move toward connection and helping others.
