## His mother became pregnant at 17 by a circus performer.

- Bezos's mother became pregnant at 17 by a circus performer; they quickly divorced.
- She remarried a Cuban immigrant.
- Jeff was interested in computer science; he graduated from Princeton, starting with physics but switching to computer science.
- In New York he found a job at a hedge fund, where he met MacKenzie – he married her.
- He left the fund and founded Amazon.com, initially focusing on selling books.
- He founded a company to build reusable spacecraft (Blue Origin).
- He purchased The Washington Post.
- Key lessons according to Bezos:
  - Focus on the long-term perspective.
  - Give your heart to customers.
  - Avoid PowerPoint.
  - Focus on important strategic decisions.
  - Hire the right people.
