# Content

- The core idea: instead of voting for politicians, citizens could use personalized AI avatars to represent their views and vote on their behalf.
- These AI representatives would be trained on each citizen's preferences, values and opinions — acting as a direct, continuous delegate in democratic processes.
- This approach aims to bypass the distortions introduced by professional politicians who may not faithfully represent their constituents.
- It shifts democracy from a representative model (vote every few years) to a more participatory, real-time model.
- The proposal challenges traditional notions of leadership and governance by distributing political agency to individual citizens.
- Technology — particularly advances in AI — makes this concept increasingly feasible, even if it raises important ethical and practical questions.
