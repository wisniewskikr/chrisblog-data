## II. Why you can't move forward

### 6. Emotions, thoughts and predictions

- Emotions are physically felt feelings.
- Anxiety: worrying about what might happen and therefore avoiding it.
- Anger: energy and an impulse to fight.
- Fear: energy and an impulse to flee or freeze.
- Shame and guilt: an impulse to correct behaviour.
- Our perception of the world depends on predictions – the brain is always one step ahead.
- Predictions are based on experience and create emotions; the brain continuously updates them.
- Shame: I am bad; guilt: I did something bad.
- Ignoring emotions makes them stay; fighting them causes a counter-attack; accepting them lets them go.

## 8. Ways of coping that only make things worse

- Unhelpful ways of dealing with reality: trying to control every situation (preventing suffering) and avoiding suffering through alcohol, drugs, etc.
- Addiction provides only a temporary window of relief – then reality returns.
- The brain dislikes fluctuations: when we regularly supply it with a substance, it stops producing it on its own, so eventually we need that substance just to feel normal.
