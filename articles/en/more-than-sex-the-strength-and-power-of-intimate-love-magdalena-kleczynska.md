# Content

## I. Intimate love

### 1. Love, intimacy and sex

- Love is much harder than sex.
- Levels of bond: (1) intellectual – a conscious choice; (2) emotional – emotional engagement; (3) behavioural – service to the other person.
- Threats to the bond: criticism, contempt and mutual blame.
- Talking about one's needs is important.
- Love should neither over-idealise nor reject feelings – a golden mean is advised.
- Components of love: (1) intimacy – honesty, respect, exchange of intimate information; (2) passion – the ability to experience strong emotions; (3) commitment – thoughts and actions for a lasting bond.
- Types of sex: (1) limited sex – relieving sexual tension; (2) comforting sex – confirming one's own attractiveness and admiration in the other person's eyes, easing the fear of a closer relationship; (3) intimate love – inner coherence, emotional engagement and deep mutual understanding.

## 2. Does marriage have to be a sexual slippery slope?

- Compatibility is a process that leads to mutual understanding.
- A crisis is not a bad thing – it is a time of change that can either destroy a relationship or bring it to a higher level.
- Happy couples attribute only 15–20% of importance to sex.
- Marriage develops just as the body does.
- The Creativity Wheel: (1) spontaneity – choosing between the familiar and the new; (2) preparation – gathering information and seeking solutions; (3) decision – taking action; (4) creativity – adjusting communication, showing tenderness and acceptance; (5) meeting – consolidating the chosen path.
- The goal of marriage is not compatibility but building a bond based on accepting the truth about oneself and one's partner.

## 3. Help! The Church is peeking into our bedroom!

- According to the Church, love should be human, complete, faithful, exclusive and fruitful.
- Talking about dreams, desires and tender spots is important.
