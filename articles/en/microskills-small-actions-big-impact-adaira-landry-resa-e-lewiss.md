# Content

## 1. Microskills related to self-care
- Nurturing relationships with people you respect and trust.
- Practising gratitude.
- Taking care of sleep.
- Mindful rest.
- Managing finances.
- Monitoring health.
- Delegating routine tasks: automation, delegation, outsourcing, and simplification.
- Organising tasks.
- Limiting time.

## 2. Microskills related to managing a task list
- Identifying tasks.
- Setting priorities.
- Estimating resources.
- Creating a schedule: Parkinson's Law – the more time we have for a task, the more time it takes.
- Adjusting deadlines.
- Identifying shared tasks.
- Defining the purpose of meetings.

## 3. Microskills related to effective communication
- Using body language mindfully.
- Recognising language barriers and communication breakdowns.
- Getting to the point without beating around the bush.
- Strategic use of storytelling.
- Less talking, more listening.
- Using asynchronous communication.
- Writing emails that recipients will actually read.
- Mastering email tools.
- Communicating negative emotions thoughtfully.
- Scheduling and sending emails during working hours.

## 4. Microskills related to building and maintaining reputation
- Meeting deadlines and communicating with the team.
- Being mindful about complaining.
- Talking about mistakes as a sign of humanity.
- Awareness of the significant impact of lies.
- Awareness of how you are perceived by others.
- Nurturing your reputation at the local level.
- Taking a thoughtful approach to your own actions.
- Keeping ego in check.
- Limiting damage when a bad reputation arises.
- Apologising thoughtfully and explaining your own mistakes.

## 5. Microskills related to deepening expertise
- Brainstorming.
- Using multimedia.
- Testing knowledge on acquaintances.
- Setting measurable benchmarks.
- Collaborating with others.
- Regular practice.
- Building a presence on social media.
- Role analysis.
- Applying for awards.
- Learning to develop knowledge.

## 6. Microskills related to absorbing workplace culture
- Introducing yourself and addressing others by name.
- Knowing the communication styles of others.
- Knowing the reporting structure in your organisation.
- Familiarising yourself with contracts, guides, handbooks, and HR policies.
- Awareness of the importance of self-control.
- Awareness of a privileged position.
- Standing up for yourself and your team.
- Mindful socialising in the workplace.
- Recognising how to handle inappropriate behaviour.

## 7. Microskills related to teamwork
- Checking in with team members.
- Appreciating the effort and contributions of team members.
- Building and strengthening psychological safety.
- Supporting people who are considering leaving.
- Avoiding scheduling meetings during others' non-working hours.
- Using sick leave when your health prevents you from working.
- Giving kind and practical feedback.
- Asking for specific feedback.
- Maintaining a positive atmosphere in the workplace.

## 8. Microskills related to expanding a network of contacts
- Identifying networking needs.
- Awareness of the difference between a conversation and a commitment.
- Overcoming discomfort: shy – feels anxiety; introvert – loses energy.
- Building relationships.
- Leveraging contacts for new opportunities.
- Demonstrating expertise.
- Avoiding faux pas.
- Sharing your network with others.

## 9. Microskills related to conflict resolution
- Learning to recognise conflicts.
- Understanding your own role in a conflict.
- Providing colleagues with a sense of psychological safety.
- Obtaining feedback, considering it, and taking further steps.
- Learning to interact with people who have difficult personalities.
- Consciously practising and having difficult conversations.
- Balancing competence with confidence.
- Keeping documentation.
- Determining whose side HR is on.
- Reporting and ignoring inappropriate behaviour.

## 10. Microskills related to actively seeking new opportunities
- Learning to transfer skills.
- Cataloguing professional development opportunities.
- Feeling comfortable talking about yourself.
- Putting yourself forward for opportunities beyond your current reach.
- Writing your own recommendation letters.
- Accepting opportunities without rushing.
- Taking care of your team while pursuing new opportunities.
