## 1. How to truly break the mould

- Machine learning involves feeding an algorithm large amounts of data, from which it creates a pattern.
- Supervised learning: we know the outcome and program the algorithm to achieve it (so-called boxes) – e.g. in recruitment we check whether a candidate meets specific criteria.
- Unsupervised learning: we do not know the outcome and look for a pattern (so-called trees) – e.g. in recruitment we examine the candidate's characteristics.

## 2. How to accept your own weirdness

- Different types of proteins perform different functions – the key is diversity, not uniformity.

## 3. How to forget about perfection

- First law of thermodynamics: energy can neither be created nor destroyed – it can only change its location and form.
- Second law of thermodynamics: in spontaneous processes, entropy (disorder) always increases.
- Bringing order requires energy input from outside – it is hard work.

## 5. How to find harmony

- Interference is the mutual influence of two waves; both the amplitude and the timing matter.
- Constructive interference: the newly formed wave is a combination of the two waves.
- Destructive interference: the waves cancel each other out.
- Resonance: the influence of external factors on a wave.

## 9. How to connect with others

- Bonds can be strong or weak, temporary or permanent, based on attraction or repulsion.
- Atoms combine into molecules, molecules form proteins.
- Covalent bonds: completing the magic number of 8 electrons, e.g. CO₂.
- Ionic bonds: attraction of opposites, e.g. NaCl (sodium chloride, i.e. salt).
- Valence: the capacity to bond – e.g. oil and water will never combine.
- Types of interactions: gravitational (weakest but most universal), electromagnetic (polarity of charges and magnetism), strong nuclear (much stronger than electromagnetic, with a limited range – they prevent protons from falling apart), weak nuclear (particles change through nuclear decay, i.e. the escape of quarks).

## 11. How to be polite

- Homology: drawing conclusions from similar cases.
