## I. The body in bodyfulness

### 1. Eight core principles of bodyfulness

- **Oscillation:** the whole body and all its systems oscillate – moving back and forth. Extreme states (paralysis or aggression) are harmful.
- **Equilibrium:** the ability to pause in a given state.
- **Feedback loops:** parts of the body communicate with one another.
- **Energy conservation:** resources are limited; habits allow us to conserve them, though habits can also be harmful.
- **Discipline:** building and maintaining good habits requires practice and discipline. The body and mind operate on a "use it or lose it" principle.
- **Change and challenge:** the way we use the body changes its structure.
- **Contrast and novelty.**
- **Associations and emotions.**
