# Content

## 1. A stranger everywhere

- Słowacki was born in Krzemieniec; his mother was Salomea Becu, who kept her son closely tied to her.
- He danced and boxed.
- He left Krzemieniec at the age of 20 and studied law in Vilnius.
- He moved to Warsaw, then to London and Paris.
- He flirted with the Pinard ladies in order to pay less for the printing of his works.
- He went to Geneva, spent time in the Alps, and travelled to Italy.
- He met Zygmunt Krasiński.
- He travelled to the Middle East.
- He died in France; the transfer of his remains became a great public demonstration.
