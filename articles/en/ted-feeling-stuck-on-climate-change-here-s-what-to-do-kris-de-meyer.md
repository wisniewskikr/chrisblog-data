# Content

- Doing something changes your mind — this is the key to not feeling stuck. Actions shape beliefs, not the other way around.
- Fear motivates only some people; for others it is demotivating or even leads to denial.
- When it comes to facts and arguments, what we think we are saying is not always what others hear.
- **Step 1.** When we disagree with someone, our mind tells us they are stupid, ignorant, or bad. We should not listen to that voice — let go of judgment.
- **Step 2.** When we disagree with someone, ask: "What do you mean when you say X?" instead of immediately pushing back.
- **Step 3.** Drop the assumption that conviction must come before action. Action can come first — and belief changes afterward.
