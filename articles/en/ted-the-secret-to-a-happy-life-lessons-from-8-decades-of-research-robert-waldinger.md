# Content
- The strongest predictor of happiness in life is having warm, strong relationships with others.
- Strong relationships make both our mind and body healthier — most likely by reducing stress.
- Conversation helps us exit the fight-or-flight mode; the absence of connection keeps us stuck in it for longer.
- It is crucial to have at least one person you can call when you are sick and need immediate help.
- When people reflect on their lives, they are most often proud of something relationship-related: being a good spouse, a good friend, or raising good children.
- Even brief conversations with strangers provide a small but real boost to our well-being.
- **How to strengthen relationships:**
  1. Be proactive — initiate meetings, make phone calls, reach out first.
  2. Build a routine of regular contact with the people who matter most to you.
  3. Do something new together with people you already see every day.
  4. Share a common interest with a group of people.
  5. Initiate and take part in casual conversations as often as possible.
