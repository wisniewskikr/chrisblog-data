# Content

- Eating more vegetables improves both physical and mental well-being — and it's also better for the planet.
- Choose quality over quantity when buying things: fewer, better items bring more satisfaction and less waste.
- A clean, organized home (feng shui your fridge!) makes you feel happier and helps you make healthier, lower-carbon food choices.
- Carpooling and sharing rides with more people reduces emissions and can boost your social connection and mood.
- Behavioral scientist Jiaying Zhao argues that climate action should feel good — positive emotions make sustainable habits stick.
- Small, joyful lifestyle changes can meaningfully lower your carbon footprint without requiring sacrifice.
