# Content

## Part I

### 1. Gloria and Joe

- Joe Kennedy, father of Jack and Robert, made his fortune in the alcohol trade and was a man of dubious morality.
- He had three sons: the eldest, Joe Junior, Jack, and Robert.
- He brought the biggest star of the era, Gloria Swanson, into his home – she was his mistress.
- On a boat, Jack spies on his father with his mistress.

## 2. Norma Jane gets married

- Marilyn Monroe's real name was Norma Jane. Her mother and grandmother were in a psychiatric hospital; she herself was passed between foster families and was sometimes molested.
- At 16 she married Jim Dougherty, who used to drive her to school – out of necessity, because her foster family could no longer support her.
- During the war Jim served in the merchant navy while Norma worked in a factory painting aircraft fuselages.
- Norma discovers her sexuality and has a strong effect on men.
- She meets David Conover, a photographer scouting attractive girls for pictures in military magazines.

## 3. Jack and Inga Binga

- Roosevelt appointed Joe Kennedy ambassador to London, wanting to keep him at a distance.
- Joe busied himself with the alcohol trade instead of supporting the war effort – for instance, he offloaded wounded soldiers to make room for alcohol. Roosevelt removed him from the post, ending his political ambitions.
- John Kennedy, a junior lieutenant in the Navy, worked in intelligence, gathering information on other countries.
- He had no great ambitions of his own – he was guided by his sister Kathleen, known as Kick, three years his junior.
- Jack met a married Danish woman, Inga Arvad, three years his senior, who wrote a society column for a newspaper. Hitler had given her two interviews, suggesting her Nazi connections.
- As punishment for the relationship with Inga, Jack was transferred to California, to Charleston.
- Despite poor health, Joe sent his son to war. Jack became commander of patrol torpedo boat PT-109, which was torpedoed. According to the official account, Jack emerged from the incident as a hero.

## 4. The meeting

- Norma married Joe DiMaggio, a baseball star.
- After Joe Junior's death in the war, John Kennedy became first in line for a political career – he became a senator and married Jackie Kennedy.
- He first met Marilyn at Charlie Feldman's in Hollywood; Feldman was a well-known talent agent.

## 5. Windswept song

- Marilyn and Jack become lovers.
- Jack suffers from Addison's disease and has constant back pain; he underwent a very risky operation.

## 6. Hollywood Confidential

- John gets rid of his crutches.
- With the help of a ghostwriter he wrote a book that won the Pulitzer Prize – the public did not know he had not written it himself.
- Marilyn had three abortions.

## 7. Poor Prince

- Robert Kennedy, as a prosecutor, fights illegal activities in trade unions.
- His main enemy is Jimmy Hoffa, who leads the unions and has ties to the Mafia.
- Monroe married for the third time, to Arthur Miller, but continues her affair with John Kennedy.
- John is on a yacht with mistresses while Jackie has a miscarriage.
- Jackie receives a million dollars to stay married to John.
- Jackie sees actor William Holden.

## 8. Some like it show

- Someone rents an apartment for Kennedy's assistant and leaks the information to the press.
- Monroe filmed "Some Like It Hot".

## 9. Danger zone

- Castro becomes the leader of Cuba.
- Alicia Darr blackmails JFK.
- JFK wins the Democratic nomination.

## 10. At odds with life

- Through Judy Campbell, John passes 250,000 dollars to Giancana, a Chicago gangster – the money is for financing the election campaign.

## Part II

### 11. The scent of a woman

- Kennedy becomes president.
- Sinatra organises the inaugural gala.
- Marilyn gets divorced.

## 12. Strange crazy woman

- Marilyn is committed to a psychiatric institution.
- Kennedy has sex with girls in the White House swimming pool.
- DiMaggio frees Marilyn from the psychiatric institution.
- Kennedy grapples with the Bay of Pigs disaster: 1,400 men were supposed to launch an uprising in Cuba; 1,200 were captured.
- Monroe ends up in another hospital and leaves six weeks later in better condition.
- Kennedy sends 200,000 American advisers to Vietnam.

## 13. Storm over Washington

- Hoover, head of the FBI, claimed the Mafia did not exist, while in reality he was in its service.
- He was fond of horse racing – that was where money was passed to him.
- Fox, the studio with which Marilyn had signed a contract, ran into enormous financial trouble because of the film "Cleopatra" with Elizabeth Taylor and effectively went bankrupt.

## 14. Mother interferes

- Joe Kennedy suffers a stroke.
- The Mafia and the CIA share a common enemy – the Kennedys.
- Kennedy faces a crisis when the pilot of the spy plane U-2 is shot down; he must arrange the exchange of a captured Russian spy.

## 15. Marilyn runs away

- Marilyn has an affair with Robert Kennedy.
- John has an affair with Mary Pinchot Meyer and makes no secret of it.
- Kennedy turns 45; Marilyn leaves the film set against Fox's orders to sing for him on his birthday. Jackie and the children are absent – they are in the countryside.

## 16. Birthday present

- Monroe sang "Happy Birthday" dressed in a transparent gown made of many layers of silk.
- The humiliated Jackie gives Kennedy an ultimatum: either he gets rid of Marilyn or he can forget about the next election.
- Marilyn is fired from Fox.
- Marilyn loses control of herself more and more – she threatens to speak publicly about John and Bobby.
- Marilyn dies by the telephone.

## 17. The big clean-up

- All traces relating to Monroe were erased.
- Kennedy is killed in Dallas.

## Epilogue

- Kennedy's mistress, Mary Pinchot Meyer, was shot dead a year later.
