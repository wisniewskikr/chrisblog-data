Article titles for a book (category books):

* Polish title (pl): "<full Polish title> – <author>". Take the full Polish title from "Book facts" (Title), including its subtitle, and the author exactly as in "Book facts" (Author). With several authors, join them with ", ".
* English title (en): "<English title> – <author>". Use the original title only when "Book facts" gives one ("Original title"). When it says "Original title: unknown", translate the Polish title literally into English — never use the title of an English edition you think you know, it may be another author's book. Never leave the English title in Polish. When the original title is in another language than English (e.g. Spanish "Enamórate de ti", German, French), translate it into English — the English title, and so the slug, must always be English. The author's name stays the same as in the Polish title.
* The separator between the title and the author is always " – " (a space, an en dash, a space). Never use ";" or ":" for it.
* Every title ends with the author's name. Do not put anything after the author.
* In the Polish title use Polish quotation marks „…” (opening „ low, closing ” high), never straight quotes "…" — e.g. Jak zmienić „ty” i „ja” w „my”. In the English title use "…".
