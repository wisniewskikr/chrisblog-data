Article titles for a book (category books):

* Polish title (pl): "<full Polish title> – <author>". Take the full Polish title from "Book facts" (Title), including its subtitle, and the author exactly as in "Book facts" (Author). With several authors, join them with ", ".
* English title (en): "<English title> – <author>". Use the official English title of the book when you know it (see "Original title" in "Book facts"); otherwise translate the Polish title into English. Never leave the English title in Polish. The author's name stays the same as in the Polish title.
* The separator between the title and the author is always " – " (a space, an en dash, a space). Never use ";" or ":" for it.
* Every title ends with the author's name. Do not put anything after the author.
